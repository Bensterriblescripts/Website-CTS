Wellington-based CTS specialises in premium quality business telecommunication services and solutions

Computer and Telephone Services Ltd (CTS) is 100% NZ owned, with over 25 years of experience in the industry offering phone systems and business broadband using high-quality products. Our client base is extensive, ranging from sole traders to complex nationwide call centres and government departments. 

We don't prioritise your business based on size because we know that effective telecommunications are vital for all businesses, NZ-wide.

About Us

CTS has been built on the core principle that customers should expect only the highest level of technical expertise and service. Prompt response, support and solutions are vital when your business communications are concerned. Our experienced team offer full assessments of your requirements and use our depth of knowledge to recommend a system that will be suitable now and scalable in the future.

Services

Computer and Telephone Services Ltd (CTS) offers a full range of Phone and VoIP systems and business fibre broadband. We work closely with business owners, architects, project and site managers to design, quote and complete full telecommunication installations and cabling on time and on budget, all in full compliance with Health and Safety requirements.

After-sales system support and remote maintenance is also a crucial aspect of maintaining long-term relationships with our valued clients.

Products

We use only high-quality products from leading brands, and can supply everything from the cabling to the wireless phone headset. 

Our products are chosen with reliability, effectiveness and user-comfort in mind and are all tried and tested.
