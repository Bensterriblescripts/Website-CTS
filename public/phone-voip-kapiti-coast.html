Thorough assessments ensure your VOIP or phone system and fibre broadband really meet your needs

When you are in business you need technology solutions that you can count on, and that not only meet the needs of your business now but can also be scaled as you grow. 

With years of experience in business phone systems and internet, we can carry out a thorough assessment of your current setup, your usage and costs and then develop a tailored solution for you.

CTS integrated solutions to improve your business connections


We don't just offer you the newest technology or the cheapest solution. We work closely with you to ensure your system meets your needs, for example, whether you need structured data cabling rather than a wireless system.

Once we have a solution in place, our team can supply and install all the components, from the wiring and cabling to the phone headsets.

Contact us to discuss your options. From the Cabling and WIFI right through to the phone headsets. We work directly with clients across the lower North Island and for those further afield in New Zealand, along-side a network of trusted partners.

Our Products Include:

PBX Phone Systems

Unified communications platforms for your business. CTS maintain strong relationships with an extensive range of leading, key brand PABX/PBX suppliers, our product awareness & high skill level is maintained as new products and technologies are launched. Compare features, benefits and costs on a vast range of Cloud PBX & on-premise PBX solutions, enabling you to make a fully informed choice on the correct telephone system for your budget and needs.

VoIP Systems

CTS utilise your computers, VoIP phones and Cloud platforms to provide useful solutions for your business, often at significantly less than other providers. Traditional lines (POTS) are coming to an end with the advent of more advanced VoIP telephony, Mobile phone, and Computer applications.

Some benefits of VoIP: 
  • Lower cost 
  • Low International rates 
  • No toll calls
  • High quality speech
  • Remote access / Home office / Portability
  • Call recording, Custom caller ID, Voicemail to email.

CTS Cloud

CTS Cloud provides a fully featured Unified Communications Solution platform offering future proof options, allowing you the freedom to grow, shift with remote access & home offices, allowing your team to work anywhere while maintaining the professional profile and front-end options you require.

CTS Lines & Broadband

CTS now offer Business lines & broadband at highly completive business rates that could make you significant savings. Let us take a look at your traditional phone bill - we’ll do the math and tell you how much you could be saving… it’s that simple.

CTS Data Cabling & WIFI

CTS understand the importance of a tested network installation and provide the latest in data cabling and WIFI solutions, ensuring a strong backbone for your communications and office equipment.
